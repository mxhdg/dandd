As an established member of the Alchemists' Guild, Marigold can rely on guild connections for food and lodging while traveling, and the guild will back her in minor legal disputes. In turn, she's expected to pay dues and answer the guild's calls when able.

### Artillerist Features (Subclass)

**Tool Proficiency:** Woodcarver's Tools

**Artillerist Spells:** see Spellcasting below

**Eldritch Cannon**
As an action, use woodcarver's tools to magically create a Tiny arcane cannon in an unoccupied space within 5 ft. Mechanically and physically, this *is* Marigold's mug: she sets it down, taps it once, and it unfolds into a small mobile coffee-cart-turret. HP 25 (5 × Artificer level), AC 18. Lasts until destroyed, dismissed (bonus action), or she creates another one. Can be picked up and stowed as an action (folding back down into an ordinary mug), or moved up to 15 ft as a bonus action.

Choose the cannon's mode when created:
- **Flamethrower** *(reflavored: a blast of pressurized scalding coffee/steam).* Bonus action: exhales in a 15-ft cone; each creature makes a Dex save, 2d8 fire damage on a fail, half on a success.
- **Force Ballista** *(reflavored: a concentrated espresso shot fired hard enough to knock someone back).* Bonus action: ranged spell attack against a target within 120 ft; on a hit, 2d8 force damage and the target is pushed up to 5 ft away.
- **Protector** *(reflavored: warmth and the smell of fresh coffee steadying everyone nearby).* At the start of each of Marigold's turns, each creature within 10 ft of the cannon (including her) gains temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + her Intelligence modifier.

**Arcane Firearm**
Marigold can channel spells through a spellcasting focus infused with this property; narratively, that's the same mug (whether folded up or deployed as the cannon). When she casts an Artificer spell through it, she can roll 1d8 and add it to one damage roll of that spell.

## Character Appearance & Backstory

**Age:** 27
**Height:** 3 ft 2 in **Weight:** 40 lbs
**Eyes:** Warm brown **Skin:** Sun-browned tan **Hair:** Dark, curly, perpetually escaping its tie

Marigold is a Small halfling with sun-browned skin and dark curly hair that never quite stays put. She grew up far from Shelldrake, in the town her parents resettled in after leaving it behind, but she was still raised inside the Kettlewick family trade: alchemists and coffee roasters who once ran a shop in Shelldrake for generations, before a brutal loan from a member of the empire's ruling council drove them out. Marigold apprenticed in the family's new shop, learning alchemy at her parents' elbows, before leaving home years ago to make her own way and train as an artificer. Shelldrake itself, though, is a place she knows only from her parents' stories: the shop that used to be theirs, the debt that's still theirs, a council member's name spoken like a family legend rather than a memory. She runs on more coffee than is medically advisable, which is at least easy to come by, given the family trade. When the Areiais Embetterment Society came recruiting agents for the Shelldrake assignment, she volunteered before they'd finished the pitch: a chance to finally see the place her family came from, and to open the first true Kettlewick's location back in the town that started it all, soon to be flooded with laborers who'll want their morning fix.

## Allies & Organizations

**Alchemists' Guild**: Marigold's guild affiliation from her Guild Artisan background; see Guild Membership under Features & Traits for the mechanical benefit.

**Areiais Embetterment Society**: the organization that recruited Marigold for the Shelldrake assignment. She doesn't fully trust it (see Flaws), but volunteered before they'd finished the pitch.

**Kettlewick's & the family debt**: The Kettlewicks were once a Shelldrake family: alchemists and coffee roasters who ran a shop in the town for generations. Two generations back, they took a loan from a member of the empire's ruling council to expand the family shop into a proper café chain. The loan's repayment terms turned out to be brutal: a standing cut of profits in perpetuity, escalating quotas, and franchise fees. Rather than stay and fight it, Marigold's parents pulled up stakes and left Shelldrake before Marigold was born, resettling elsewhere and rebuilding the trade from scratch, still owing on a debt to a town they no longer called home.

Marigold grew up on her parents' stories about Shelldrake, never having set foot in the town herself. Its rebuild is, to her, a market opportunity as much as a mission: cheap real estate, a captive audience of laborers and outsiders in need of their morning fix, and a chance to open the first true Kettlewick's location back in the town that started it all, finally clearing a debt that's outlived the people who took it on.

## Treasure: Marigold's Mug

*No companion creature. Her "power source" is a single infused item that does double duty, not a homunculus.*

The mug has two states:

**1. Everyday state: Enhanced Arcane Focus infusion.** While holding it, Marigold gains a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls made with it (scales to +2 at Artificer level 10, +3 at level 18). Already folded into the Spell Attack Bonus below.

**2. Deployed state: Eldritch Cannon.** Set down and tapped, it unfolds into her Artillerist turret (Flamethrower / Force Ballista / Protector modes, reflavored as scalding coffee/steam). Full stats and modes are under Artillerist Features above. It's also the focus she channels Arcane Firearm through, adding 1d8 to a spell's damage roll when cast through it.

**Origin:** Enchanted years ago by her parents, using the family's alchemical know-how, as a parting gift when Marigold left home to strike out on her own. Marigold's parents' original gift was the Enhanced Arcane Focus enchantment; Marigold herself later layered the cannon and firearm properties onto it as she learned Artillerist tricks, so it grew with her rather than being one single overpowered heirloom.
