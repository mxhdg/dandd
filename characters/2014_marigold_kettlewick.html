Character Backstory
Marigold is a Small halfling with sun-browned skin and dark curly hair that never quite stays put. She grew up far from Shelldrake, in the town her parents resettled in after leaving it behind, but she was still raised inside the Kettlewick family trade: alchemists and coffee roasters who once ran a shop in Shelldrake for generations, before a brutal loan from a member of the empire's ruling council drove them out. Marigold apprenticed in the family's new shop, learning alchemy at her parents' elbows, before leaving home years ago to make her own way and train as an artificer. Shelldrake itself, though, is a place she knows only from her parents' stories: the shop that used to be theirs, the debt that's still theirs, a council member's name spoken like a family legend rather than a memory. She runs on more coffee than is medically advisable, which is at least easy to come by, given the family trade. When the Areiais Embetterment Society came recruiting agents for the Shelldrake assignment, she volunteered before they'd finished the pitch: a chance to finally see the place her family came from, and to open the first true Kettlewick's location back in the town that started it all, soon to be flooded with laborers who'll want their morning fix.
Allies & Organizations
Alchemists' Guild — her Guild Artisan background affiliation; guild backs her in minor legal disputes.

Areiais Embetterment Society — recruited her for the Shelldrake assignment. She doesn't fully trust it, but volunteered before they'd finished the pitch.

Kettlewick's & the family debt — the Kettlewicks once ran a café-chain shop in Shelldrake, financed by a brutal loan from a member of the ruling council. Her parents left Shelldrake before she was born rather than keep fighting the debt; she's returning to a town she's never actually lived in, hoping to finally clear it.
Treasure — Marigold's Mug
A battered tin coffee mug with two states. Everyday: Enhanced Arcane Focus infusion, +1 to spell attack rolls while held (already included in Spell Attack Bonus). Deployed: set down and tapped, it unfolds into her Eldritch Cannon turret. Also channels her Arcane Firearm bonus damage. Enchanted by her parents as a parting gift when she left home; she's since layered on the cannon/firearm properties herself.
