## Features & Traits

### Racial Traits (Half-Elf)

**Darkvision:** 60 ft
**Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saves vs. being charmed; magic can't put you to sleep
**Skill Versatility:** Proficiency in two additional skills (Animal Handling, Perception)

### Fighter Features

**Fighting Style: Archery**
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with ranged weapons.

**Second Wind** (1/Short Rest)
Bonus action to regain 1d10 + 3 hit points.

**Action Surge** (1/Short Rest)
On your turn, you can take one additional action.

**Martial Archetype: Battle Master**

**Superiority Dice:** 4d8 (regain all on short or long rest)
**Maneuver Save DC:** 12 (8 + proficiency bonus + Dex modifier)

**Maneuvers Known:**
- **Parry** — When damaged by a melee attack, use your reaction to reduce damage by 1d8 + 3.
- **Riposte** — When a creature misses you with a melee attack, use your reaction to make one melee weapon attack against it. Add superiority die to damage.
- **Trip Attack** — When you hit with a weapon attack, add superiority die to damage and target must succeed on Str save or be knocked prone.

### Ranger Features

**Favored Enemy: Beasts**
Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track beasts and Intelligence checks to recall information about them.

**Natural Explorer: Forest**
- Difficult terrain doesn't slow your group's travel
- Your group can't become lost except by magical means
- Remain alert to danger even when foraging, tracking, or navigating
- Move stealthily at normal pace when alone
- Find twice as much food when foraging
- When tracking, learn exact number, sizes, and how long ago creatures passed

## Companion — Doug (Fire-Breathing Rabbit)

**⚠️ Homebrew/DM-Approved Content**

**Armor Class:** 13
**Hit Points:** 12
**Speed:** 30 ft, burrow 10 ft

**Abilities:**
- **Fire Breath** (1/Long Rest): Exhales fire in a 10-ft cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
- **Fiery Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: 1d6 piercing damage plus 1d6 fire damage.

**Personality:** Mischievous and fiercely loyal to Elowen.
