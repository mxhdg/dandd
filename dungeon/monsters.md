# MONSTER STAT BLOCKS FOR THE VANILLA VAULT

Below are stat blocks for:
1. Shadow Beast (Entrance, Innistrad draw)
2. Cursed Extract Spirit (Hall of Extracts, Innistrad draw)
3. Vanilla Golem (Cold Cream Chamber)
4. Custard Warden (Boss)

All stat blocks are original and 5e compatible.

---

# SHADOW BEAST
Medium aberration, chaotic evil

**Armor Class** 13  
**Hit Points** 27 (6d8)  
**Speed** 40 ft

**STR** 14 (+2)  
**DEX** 16 (+3)  
**CON** 12 (+1)  
**INT** 6 (-2)  
**WIS** 12 (+1)  
**CHA** 6 (-2)

**Skills** Stealth +5  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic  
**Damage Vulnerabilities** radiant  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft  
**Languages** understands Common but cannot speak  
**Challenge** 1

### Shadow Pounce
The beast can move up to 20 feet as a bonus action if it starts its turn in dim light or darkness.

### Actions

**Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target.  
Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

**Shadow Howl (Recharge 5 to 6).**  
Each creature within 15 feet must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom save or be frightened until the end of its next turn.

# CURSED EXTRACT SPIRIT
Small undead, neutral evil

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 18 (4d6 + 4)  
**Speed** 0 ft, fly 40 ft (hover)

**STR** 4 (-3)  
**DEX** 14 (+2)  
**CON** 12 (+1)  
**INT** 10 (+0)  
**WIS** 12 (+1)  
**CHA** 14 (+2)

**Damage Resistances** necrotic, cold  
**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** poisoned, prone  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft  
**Languages** understands all languages it knew in life  
**Challenge** 1

### Essence Drain
When the spirit hits a creature with an attack, the target’s next ability check is made with disadvantage.

### Actions

**Chilling Touch.** Melee Spell Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft.  
Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) cold damage.

**Extract Scream (Recharge 6).**  
Each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 12 Constitution save or take 7 (2d6) psychic damage and be rattled, giving disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes.

# VANILLA GOLEM
Medium construct, unaligned

**Armor Class** 15 (frozen shell)  
**Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)  
**Speed** 25 ft

**STR** 16 (+3)  
**DEX** 10 (+0)  
**CON** 16 (+3)  
**INT** 3 (-4)  
**WIS** 10 (+0)  
**CHA** 5 (-3)

**Damage Resistances** cold, bludgeoning  
**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, poisoned  
**Senses** blindsight 30 ft  
**Languages** understands the language of its creator  
**Challenge** 3

### Frost Core
When the golem is hit by a melee attack, the attacker takes 3 (1d6) cold damage.

### Sweet Aroma
Creatures within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom save or have disadvantage on attacks against the golem until the end of their next turn.

### Actions

**Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.  
Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.

**Frozen Burst (Recharge 5 to 6).**  
The golem releases a burst of icy mist.  
Each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 13 Constitution save or take 9 (2d8) cold damage and have its speed reduced by 10 feet for 1 round.

# CUSTARD WARDEN
Large elemental, lawful neutral

**Armor Class** 16  
**Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)  
**Speed** 30 ft, hover 20 ft

**STR** 18 (+4)  
**DEX** 12 (+1)  
**CON** 16 (+3)  
**INT** 10 (+0)  
**WIS** 14 (+2)  
**CHA** 16 (+3)

**Damage Resistances** cold, radiant  
**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** poisoned, restrained  
**Senses** tremorsense 30 ft  
**Languages** Celestial, Common  
**Challenge** 5

### Aura of Sweetness
Creatures that start their turn within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom save or be charmed until the end of their turn.

### Custard Form
The Warden can move through spaces as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.

### Actions

**Cream Lash.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft.  
Hit: 12 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

**Radiant Glaze (Recharge 5 to 6).**  
The Warden emits a burst of glowing custard.  
Creatures within 15 feet must make a DC 14 Dexterity save or take 14 (4d6) radiant damage and be blinded until the end of their next turn.

### Legendary Action (1 per round)

**Stabilizing Pulse.**  
The Warden emits a pulse of dream realm energy.  
One creature of its choice regains 5 hit points or ends one condition affecting it.
