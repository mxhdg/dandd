# Dream Encounter: Night Hag False BBEG

## Overview
The party is pulled into a shared dream after a lightning flash. A Night Hag, glamoured to appear harmless, tries to stop them from continuing their real-world quest. She cannot reveal her true motives. The encounter includes dream bleed moments from each character, a glamour reveal, a staged unwinnable fight, and a forced awakening.

---

# 1. The Storm and Dreamfall
A violent storm rolls in. A flash of lightning swallows everything. Sound cuts out.

The characters "wake" standing together on the same country road. The wagon, supplies, and animal companions are gone. The world feels still and wrong.

Let them explore and talk. The dream logic should feel slightly off.

---

# 2. The Glamoured Hag Appears
The Night Hag uses Disguise Self or Illusory Appearance to appear as a familiar or comforting figure. Choose one that fits your table:

- A kindly traveler
- A shepherd with a goat
- A stagehand from the Bard's past
- A halfling grandmother figure
- A merchant who claims to have seen their wagon

She warns them gently.

> "You should not be here. The road ahead is wrong. Turn back before it takes you."

She is evasive if questioned. If they push forward, the dream destabilizes.

---

# 3. Dream Bleed: Bard's Missing Saw
The world shifts into a backstage performance hall. Both characters are present.

- The Bard's saw is missing.
- The Fighter sees the environment too and can help search.
- Props and objects behave like dream logic.

The glamoured hag appears as a stage manager urging them to leave.

Her glamour flickers:
- Her shadow looks wrong.
- Her voice echoes.
- Her eyes flash red for a moment.

When they push forward, the scene collapses back to the dream road.

---

# 4. Dream Bleed: Fighter's Goat Teeth
The world shifts into a grassy field. Both characters are present.

- The goat trots up with the Fighter's teeth.
- The Bard sees the goat's mouth moving like a puppet.
- The goat tries to communicate something symbolic.

The glamoured hag appears as a shepherd, warning them again.

Her glamour cracks further:
- Her hands look clawed for a heartbeat.
- Her silhouette warps.
- Her voice becomes layered.

When they push forward, the scene collapses back to the dream road.

---

# 5. Glamour Breaks
When they refuse to turn back, the hag can no longer maintain the illusion.

The glamour shatters like glass.

She stands revealed:
- Blue-gray skin
- Burning coal eyes
- Clawed hands
- Hair floating as if underwater
- A smell of cold iron and grave dirt

She says:

> "I tried to warn you. You walk toward your doom. I cannot let you continue."

Combat begins.

---

# 6. Combat Phase One (Winnable)
Use the standard Night Hag stat block.

She fights cautiously:
- Claw attacks
- Ray of Enfeeblement
- Magic Missile
- Sleep
- Psychic flavored effects

She deals non-lethal damage unless they escalate.

Let them feel like they can win. Let them get her to half HP.

---

# 7. Combat Phase Two (Unwinnable)
At roughly 30 HP, the dream begins to break.

Environmental shifts:
- Stage floor
- Grass field
- Country road
- All three layered together

Weapons distort. Voices echo. Movement feels slowed.

Hag behavior:
- Short teleports (flavored Etherealness)
- Intangible between turns
- Harder to hit (narrative, not mechanical)
- Overlapping voices

At 15 HP, she stops taking damage. Attacks pass through her like smoke.

She says:

> "You cannot defeat me. You were never meant to. Wake up before he finds you."

This signals the unwinnable moment.

---

# 8. The Dream Ends
When both characters are:
- Below half HP
- Exhausted
- Or verbally give up
- Or assert their will symbolically

The hag claps her hands. A shockwave of white light erupts. The dream shatters.

---

# 9. Awakening
They wake under the wagon. The storm is gone. Everything is normal.

But each character finds an impossible artifact.

- Bard: A metal shaving shaped like a musical note.
- Fighter: A goat tooth with a faint human-tooth engraving.

These objects prove the dream was real.

---

# Notes for Improv
- The hag never reveals her true motive.
- The dream logic can be as surreal as you want.
- The environment can shift at any time.
- The hag's warnings should sound ominous but protective.
- The fight should feel dangerous but never lethal.
