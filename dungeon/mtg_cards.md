# MAGIC: THE GATHERING → D&D CONVERSION SYSTEM

A fast, improv‑friendly system for turning any MTG card into a D&D effect.  
Designed for use with Innistrad: Midnight Hunt and BloomBurrow booster packs.

---

## 1. COLOR IDENTITY → EFFECT FLAVOR

Use the card’s color(s) to determine the theme of the effect.

### White
- Protection  
- Healing  
- Clarity  
- Order  
Examples: radiant light, shields, cleansing magic

### Blue
- Illusion  
- Knowledge  
- Control  
- Foresight  
Examples: illusions, divination, mind tricks

### Black
- Curses  
- Necrotic energy  
- Fear  
- Sacrifice  
Examples: life drain, shadow magic, debuffs

### Red
- Fire  
- Chaos  
- Emotion  
- Sudden action  
Examples: flame bursts, reckless boosts, wild magic

### Green
- Nature  
- Growth  
- Beasts  
- Physical enhancement  
Examples: vines, animal spirits, regeneration

### Multicolor
Blend the themes.

### Colorless
Weird, cosmic, artifact‑like effects.

---

## 2. CARD TYPE → CATEGORY OF EFFECT

This determines what kind of effect happens.

### Creature
- Summon a creature  
- Grant a physical buff  
- Manifest an animal or spirit  

### Instant / Sorcery
- One‑time magical effect  
- Burst of energy  
- Spell‑like action  

### Enchantment
- Ongoing aura  
- Persistent buff or debuff  
- Environmental shift  

### Artifact
- Temporary magic item  
- Mechanical device activates  
- Puzzle or contraption effect  

### Land
- Terrain changes  
- Environmental magic  
- Natural hazards or blessings  

### Planeswalker
- Major NPC intervention  
- Multi‑round magical influence  

---

## 3. MANA VALUE → POWER LEVEL

Use the mana value (top right of the card) to scale the effect.

### 0–2 Mana
Minor effect  
- Small buff  
- Tiny creature  
- Light environmental change  

### 3–4 Mana
Moderate effect  
- Useful spell  
- CR 1–3 creature  
- Room hazard  

### 5–6 Mana
Strong effect  
- CR 4–6 creature  
- Party‑wide buff  
- Major magical surge  

### 7+ Mana
Epic effect  
- Reality warp  
- Boss‑level summon  
- Room transformation  

---

## 4. SET TONE MODIFIER

The booster pack chosen determines the mood of the magic.

### Innistrad: Midnight Hunt Tone
Dark, cursed, gothic, tragic, moon‑touched.

Effects tend to be:
- Shadowy  
- Necrotic  
- Illusory  
- Corrupted  
- Fear‑based  
- Lunar‑themed  

Examples:
- A creature mutates with shadow energy  
- A curse spreads across the room  
- A ghostly figure appears  
- Moonlight warps reality  

### BloomBurrow Tone
Whimsical, nature‑bound, cozy, animal‑folk magic.

Effects tend to be:
- Helpful  
- Playful  
- Nature‑based  
- Spirit‑guided  
- Emotionally uplifting  

Examples:
- A tiny critter spirit appears  
- Vines grow to help the party  
- A burst of joy grants temporary HP  
- A woodland guardian offers advice  

---

## 5. FAST IMPROV METHOD (AT THE TABLE)

When a player draws a card:

1. Identify the color  
2. Identify the card type  
3. Identify the mana value  
4. Apply the set tone (Innistrad or BloomBurrow)  
5. Improvise a one‑sentence effect

---

## 6. EXAMPLES

### Example 1 — Innistrad Red Creature, MV 3
A fiery wolf spirit bursts from the shadows and attacks.

### Example 2 — BloomBurrow Green Enchantment, MV 2
A gentle aura of leaves surrounds the party, granting +1 AC for 10 minutes.

### Example 3 — Innistrad Blue Sorcery, MV 5
A wave of moonlit illusions sweeps the room; enemies must make a Wisdom save or be blinded.

### Example 4 — BloomBurrow White Creature, MV 1
A tiny mousefolk spirit appears and gives one player advantage on their next roll.
