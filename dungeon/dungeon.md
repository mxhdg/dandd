# THE VANILLA VAULT
A one session dungeon crawl that continues the night hag plot, introduces moral ambiguity, and integrates MTG booster pack magic.

This packet includes:
- Session premise
- Dungeon overview
- All 5 rooms
- Booster pack integration rules
- Ending beat
- Notes for improvisation

---

# 1. SESSION PREMISE

Last session, the party escaped a dream realm after fighting a night hag.  
The hag insisted that the person the party works for is actually the true villain, but gave few details.

Tonight is not a major reveal.  
It is a self contained dungeon crawl that:
- Moves the plot slightly
- Gives the party a new magical ingredient
- Lets you use the MTG booster mechanic
- Keeps the long term mystery alive

The dungeon is called The Vanilla Vault.

---

# 2. BOOSTER PACK MECHANIC

Players choose one of two packs:
- Innistrad Midnight Hunt (dark, cursed, gothic)
- BloomBurrow (whimsical, nature, animal folk)

Whenever the dungeon calls for a magical surge, trap, mood shift, or creature mutation, have a player draw a card.

Use these rules:

## Color Identity to Effect Flavor
White: protection, healing, clarity  
Blue: illusion, knowledge, control  
Black: curses, necrotic, fear  
Red: fire, chaos, emotion  
Green: nature, growth, beasts  
Multicolor: blend themes  
Colorless: strange, cosmic, artifact like

## Card Type to Effect Category
Creature: summon or buff  
Instant or Sorcery: one time magical effect  
Enchantment: ongoing aura or condition  
Artifact: item, device, puzzle  
Land: terrain or environment shift  
Planeswalker: major NPC style effect

## Mana Value to Power Level
0 to 2: minor  
3 to 4: moderate  
5 to 6: strong  
7 plus: epic

## Set Tone Modifier
Innistrad: dark, cursed, tragic, moon touched  
BloomBurrow: playful, nature based, cozy, uplifting

---

# 3. DUNGEON OVERVIEW

The Vanilla Vault is a forgotten underground facility built by ancient confectioner mages who crafted magical desserts to stabilize the dream realm.

The party believes this is where they will find the next ingredient for the magical vanilla ice cream.

They are correct, but the hag wants them to see the cost of the recipe.

The dungeon has 4 rooms plus a boss.

---

# 4. ROOM 1: THE FLAVOR LOCK

A massive stone door with four rotating rings carved with symbols:
- Moon
- Sun
- Beast
- Plant

A faint sweet mist leaks from the seams.

## Puzzle
A player draws a card.

Innistrad draw:
- Door opens with a dark pulse
- A shadow creature emerges (CR 1 to 2)
- Use the card color to flavor the creature

BloomBurrow draw:
- Door opens with a gentle breeze
- A tiny nature spirit appears and offers a hint

Purpose: introduce the booster mechanic immediately.

---

# 5. ROOM 2: THE HALL OF EXTRACTS

Shelves of glowing vials labeled:
- Essence of Joy
- Essence of Memory
- Essence of Fear
- Essence of Hunger

A magical extractor machine hums in the center.

## Challenge
Players must collect the correct vial to proceed.  
The machine reacts to the card they draw.

Innistrad:
- Releases a curse, illusion, or hostile spirit

BloomBurrow:
- Releases a helpful animal spirit or nature effect

Color interpretation examples:
- Red: fire burst
- Blue: knowledge or illusion
- Green: vines or growth
- Black: necrotic or fear
- White: healing or protection

---

# 6. ROOM 3: THE COLD CREAM CHAMBER

A freezing room swirling with frost.  
In the center: a pedestal holding Frost Vanilla Essence, the next ingredient.

Guarding it: a Vanilla Golem (use any CR appropriate construct stat block).

## Booster Integration
Draw a card before combat.

Innistrad:
- Golem mutates with shadow clones, necrotic aura, fear effect

BloomBurrow:
- Golem gains whimsical quirks like summoning critters or healing allies

Mana value scales the effect:
- Low MV: small buff
- Mid MV: moderate mutation
- High MV: major transformation

---

# 7. ROOM 4: THE RECIPE ARCHIVE

Ancient murals show:
- The magical dessert is a binding ritual
- It stabilizes the dream realm
- Whoever controls the dessert controls the boundary between dreams and reality
- The hag wants to break the boundary
- Their employer wants to control it

Important note:
Neither side is fully right.  
This keeps the long term mystery alive.

This room gives just enough lore to keep players thinking without revealing the truth.

---

# 8. ROOM 5: THE CUSTARD WARDEN

A guardian spirit made of swirling cream and starlight.

It speaks:
"Sweetness is power. Power is choice. Choose wisely."

## Booster Integration
Draw a card to determine the Warden's mood.

Innistrad:
- Hostile, tragic, corrupted

BloomBurrow:
- Curious, playful, protective

## Card Type to Encounter
Creature: combat  
Instant or Sorcery: magical challenge  
Enchantment: environmental hazard  
Artifact: puzzle  
Land: terrain shift

## Reward
The Warden grants:
Ingredient 2: Frost Vanilla Essence

And a cryptic warning:
"The one who sent you seeks more than flavor."

---

# 9. SESSION ENDING BEAT

As they leave the dungeon:
- The dream realm flickers
- A whisper of the hag's voice
- A shadow of their employer
- A ripple of moonlight

Just enough to keep them guessing.
