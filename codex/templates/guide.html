Getting Started

This app shows a live character sheet during a session. Static build info (class, stats, backstory) is set up ahead of time; the fields you can actually edit here are the ones that change moment to moment during play — HP, conditions, spell slots, and so on.

Opening a sheet

Pick a character from the list. Everything on the page loads from that character's last saved state.

Nothing saves until you click it. This app has no JavaScript, so edits only exist in your browser until you press Save Changes (top right). That includes condition chips, HP, spell slots — everything. Refreshing or navigating away before saving throws the change away.

Hit Points

Rather than doing the subtraction yourself, put the amount into Damage Taken or Healing Received and save — the app applies it against Current/Temporary HP for you (temp HP absorbs damage first, healing caps at your max).

Conditions

Tap a condition chip to toggle it — it turns solid immediately, no save needed to see the highlight. Saving is still what makes it stick for next time you open the sheet.

Exhaustion has its own 0–6 dropdown in the same box. The effects for your current level (and every level below it) are listed right under it.

Concentration

A plain text field for whatever spell you're concentrating on, so a Constitution save prompt doesn't send you digging through notes.

Death Saves

Check off successes and failures as they happen. Once either count hits 3 and you save, a STABILIZED or DEAD banner appears next to the checkboxes.

Spell Slots & Hit Dice

The "Used" boxes are normal number fields — use your browser's up/down arrows or type a number directly.

Short & Long Rests

The Rest box has two buttons, and pressing either one also saves anything else you've changed on the sheet.

  • Short Rest: enter how many hit dice you spent and the total healing you rolled, then press it. The dice are marked used and the healing is added to your HP (up to your max).
  • Long Rest: just press it. HP goes to max, temp HP and death saves clear, all spell slots come back, you regain half your hit dice (at least one), and exhaustion drops by one level.

Printing / exporting to PDF

Press the PDF button (top left, next to Characters). The sheet opens as a PDF in a new tab, with the same page margins in every browser. Save or print it from there. It shows your last saved values, so save first. Your browser's own Print dialog (Ctrl/Cmd+P) still works, but its margins depend on the browser.

On your phone

The sheet is usable down to a phone-sized screen — no separate mobile app needed, just open the same page.
